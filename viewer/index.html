How this was tested

For each cell, the model counts spikes after a picture appears. The window runs from 30 to 250 ms after onset. It ends sooner if the picture disappears.

Images 0, 30, 60, and 90 were picked before scoring.

Each cell count gets its own logistic regression model, with C=1. Scaling is fitted on the training trials.
